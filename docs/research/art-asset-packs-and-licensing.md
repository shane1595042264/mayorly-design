# Pixel Art Asset Packs for todoFarm

## Bottom line

Two viable routes. Pick one.

**Route A, the "$15 and done" commercial stack (recommended).** Buy the paid tiers. Every license below explicitly permits commercial use in a closed-source paid app, and none require open-sourcing.

| Need | Pack | Cost | Credit required? |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) Farm/overworld tileset | **Cute Fantasy RPG** by Kenmi, 16x16, Premium | $3.99+ | No (premium tier) |
| (b) Animated chest | **FREE RPG Pixel Art Chests w/Animation** by Serial, 18x16 top-down, 4 frames | $0 | Not stated |
| (c) 4-direction player | Included in Cute Fantasy RPG (player + customization tool) | included | No |
| (d) NPC + expressions | 8 premade NPCs in Cute Fantasy RPG; portraits from **Modern User Interface** by LimeZu | included / $3.90 | Yes for LimeZu |
| (e) Dialogue box / 9-slice panels | **Cute Fantasy UI** by Kenmi, 16x16, style-matched | $2.99 | Not stated |
| (f) Inventory slot frames | Included in Cute Fantasy UI ("Inventory UI with grid slots") | included | No |

Total roughly $7 if you stay all-Kenmi, roughly $11 if you add LimeZu's portrait generator and animated dialogue boxes.

**Route B, the zero-obligation CC0 stack.** No attribution, no license file, no risk, weaker art cohesion.

- Tileset: **Kenney Tiny Town** + **Kenney Tiny Farm**, 16x16, 130 sprites each, CC0. https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town and https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-farm
- Chest: **Pixel Art Chest Pack Animated** by karsiori, CC0, open/close + lock/unlock frames. https://karsiori.itch.io/pixel-art-chest-pack-animated
- Player + NPC + facesets + UI + tilesets, all in one: **Ninja Adventure Asset Pack** by pixel-boy, 16x16, CC0. https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack
- UI frames: **Kenney Fantasy UI Borders**, 140 assets, CC0. https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-ui-borders

**Practical recommendation:** start Route B (CC0) for the prototype so nothing blocks you, then swap in Route A art before you ship. Ninja Adventure alone covers a, c, d, e and probably f, at zero legal cost.

**Hard flags, read these:**

1. **The free tiers of Sprout Lands, Cozy Farm, and Cute Fantasy RPG explicitly forbid commercial use.** You must pay. Verbatim from Cup Nooble: "This asset pack can be used in any non-commercial project ... This asset pack can't be used in any commercial project, resold/redistributed, even if modified." (https://cupnooble.itch.io/sprout-lands-asset-pack)
2. **Avoid Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) assets entirely.** Licensed CC-BY-SA 3.0 and GPL 3.0. Share-alike/copyleft is exactly the trap for a closed-source paid app. https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles
3. **Mana Seed (Seliel the Shaper) is licensed per product.** Excellent Stardew-grade 4-direction base, but using it in a second product requires a second purchase, and print merch is prohibited. https://selieltheshaper.weebly.com/user-license.html
4. **Crusenho's UI pack is CC BY 4.0**, which is a real ongoing obligation: credit, link to the license, and state that you modified it. Fine, but it must go in an in-app credits screen.

## Packs to avoid, and why

**Liberated Pixel Cup base assets** https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles
Licensed CC-BY-SA 3.0 **and** GPL 3.0. Share-alike propagates to derivative art, and the GPL option is worse still for a shipped binary. This is the classic trap for a commercial closed-source app, and LPC art is everywhere in tutorials, so you will trip over it. Do not use it. The page notes only that some assets by Leana "Sharm" Zimmerman and Stephen "Redshrike" Challener are additionally available under OGA-BY 3.0, which is a narrow carve-out, not a general escape.

**Any free tier of Sprout Lands, Cozy Farm, or Cute Fantasy RPG.** All three carry a flat "can't be used in any commercial project" clause. Prototyping with the free tier and forgetting to upgrade is the realistic failure mode here. Either buy on day one or prototype on CC0 art.

# Adversarial fact-check

## Corrections

**1. LimeZu Modern Exteriors price is inverted.**
- Claim: "$2.50 USD, listed at $1.25 during a 50 percent sale when I checked."
- Truth: The page shows $2.50 USD as the *sale* price, 50 percent off a $5.00 base. There is no $1.25 tier. The report treated the discounted price as the list price and invented a second discount below it.
- https://limezu.itch.io/modernexteriors

**2. The Cute Fantasy RPG free tier does NOT "explicitly forbid" commercial use, and the report contradicts its own quote.**
- Claim (Hard flag 1): "The free tiers of Sprout Lands, Cozy Farm, and Cute Fantasy RPG **explicitly forbid** commercial use." Restated later: "All three carry a flat 'can't be used in any commercial project' clause."
- Truth: Sprout Lands and Cozy Farm do carry that explicit prohibition. Cute Fantasy RPG does not. Its free license reads in full: "This asset pack can be used in any non-commerical project, you may modify the assets as much as you want. This asset pack can't be resold or redistributed even if modified. Please Credit: Kenmi or use (this link)." The restriction is implicit in the scope of the grant, not an explicit prohibition clause. The report's own verbatim quote in the evidence section omits the clause, so the two sections disagree with each other.
- Practical consequence is unchanged (do not ship the free tier commercially, the grant does not cover it), but a report whose headline flag misquotes a license is not safe to lift verbatim into a legal note.
- https://kenmi-art.itch.io/cute-fantasy-rpg

**3. The Cute Fantasy UI "redistribution" clarification is a creator comment, not page or license text.**
- Claim: "**The page also clarifies** that 'redistribution' means sharing the asset pack itself, not shipping a game that contains it. That clarification is exactly what you want on record for a Tauri .app bundle."
- Truth: The license block contains no such clarification. The wording appears in the itch.io comment thread, where the creator replied: "you can make a game with it and sell it but you cant buy one copy and then send it to ten other people for them to make their own games." A comment from a creator is weaker evidence than license text, is not versioned, and can be deleted. Calling it "on record" overstates it.
- https://kenmi-art.itch.io/cute-fantasy-ui

**4. The Kenney itch.io quote is from a paid $19.95 bundle page, not a general storefront license.**
- Claim: "Kenney's own itch.io **storefront** states verbatim: 'Use the assets in unlimited commercial projects (no attribution required).'"
- Truth: The quote is accurate but it sits on the "Kenney Game Assets" product page, which is a paid bundle priced at $19.95 USD or more. It is marketing copy for a purchase, not a license covering the free kenney.nl downloads. The load-bearing evidence for the free packs is the per-pack "Creative Commons CC0" field, which I did confirm. The report should not have cited a paid-bundle page as corroboration for free-pack terms.
- https://kenney.itch.io/kenney-game-assets

**5. The Ninja Adventure GitHub repo does exist and is reachable.**
- Claim: "github.com/Pixel-Boy/NinjaAdventure and its raw README both failed to return content when I fetched them."
- Truth: The repository resolves and returns content. I still could not extract a LICENSE file quote from it, so the report's underlying caution (verify the LICENSE file in the download) stands, but the stated fetch failure is not accurate.
- https://github.com/Pixel-Boy/NinjaAdventure

**6. "Free 39 Portraits" is name-your-own-price, not flatly free.** Minor, but the report's pricing column should be consistent. https://free-game-assets.itch.io/free-39-portraits-pixel-art-game-assets

**7. LPC framing is slightly off.** The report says "Licensed CC-BY-SA 3.0 **and** GPL 3.0." OpenGameArt lists two licenses as a dual-license offer, meaning you pick one, not both simultaneously. The report's conclusion (avoid for a closed-source commercial app) is defensible and I would keep it, but "avoid entirely" is stronger than the law requires: CC-BY-SA on *art assets* obliges you to keep the art and its derivatives under share-alike with attribution, which many commercial games do ship. Presenting it as a binary trap is over-broad. https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles

## What I verified as correct

Nearly all license quotes check out word for word. Confirmed accurate against the live pages: Cute Fantasy RPG premium and free license text, $3.99 premium, 16x16, $17.50 bundle, and the "Please Credit: Kenmi" line the report quoted; Cute Fantasy UI license, $2.99 at 25 percent off $3.99, 16x16, grid slots; Sprout Lands pack and UI pack free and premium licenses, $3.99, UI pack 16x16; Cozy Farm both tiers, $3.99, $20.00 bundle, 16x16; Kenney Tiny Town and Tiny Farm (CC0, free, 130 sprites, 16x16) and Fantasy UI Borders (CC0, 140 assets, no stated dimensions or 9-slice); Cainos (license, 32x32, contents); Serial RPG chests (license, 18x16 and 18x20, 4 frames, no credit clause); karsiori (CC0, 9 chests, 40x25 to 72x62, 1 to 5 and 2 to 4 frames); OpenGameArt 16x16 chest (CC0, filename, 359 b, author note); Ninja Adventure itch license (CC0, attribution not required, 16x16, contents); Mana Seed user license on every clause quoted, including per-product scope, no-credit-required, no print, no ML datasets, no web3; LimeZu Modern User Interface ($3.90 at 35 percent off $6.00, credits required, portrait generator, 10-frame talk/nod/shake); LimeZu animated chests (license, $1 minimum, 32x16, four animations, no credit line); Crusenho (CC BY 4.0, $3.15 and $3.51 tiers, 32x32, 9-slice, 800+, 13 themes, Toriko font); CraftPix freebie and paid clauses as quoted; LPC dual license and the Sharm/Redshrike OGA-BY 3.0 carve-out; Free 39 Portraits showing no license text at all.

The report's central legal conclusion, that Route A is commercially safe on paid tiers and Route B is CC0-clean, is correct.

## Unverifiable claims

- **Kenney's central license document.** kenney.nl/license does return HTTP 404, so the report is right. I went further: there is no license article in the Knowledge Base (https://kenney.nl/knowledge-base) and https://kenney.nl/terms-of-service is silent on asset licensing. The per-pack "Creative Commons CC0" field is genuinely the only authority. The report's gap is real and I could not close it.
- **Cute Fantasy RPG "player customization tool."** The page describes customization *options* (clothes, hairstyles) in the premium tier. I could not confirm a standalone generator tool ships, unlike LimeZu's, which is explicitly named. Treat as unverified.
- **Whether the fonts bundled inside Cute Fantasy UI, Crusenho, and Ninja Adventure are covered by the same license as the art.** Not stated on any of the three pages. See Omissions.
- **The four chest packs and eleven UI/portrait packs the report itself listed as unverified.** I did not open them either. Its disclosure there is honest and I am not upgrading any of them.
- **LimeZu credit requirement consistency.** Modern Exteriors and Modern User Interface both print "Credits required." Modern Farm's page reads as credits *appreciated*. Same vendor, different wording, so credit LimeZu regardless.

## Omissions

**1. LimeZu Modern Farm exists and the report missed it.** This is the most consequential gap. The report recommends LimeZu Modern *Exteriors* and then says "This is urban, not rural, so it fits a farm world poorly; listed because it is the sibling of the UI pack recommended below." LimeZu ships **Modern Farm, RPG Tileset [16x16]**: $4.95 at 34 percent off $7.50, ships 16x16 / 32x32 / 48x48, barns, fences, animated gates and sprinklers, 19 crops with growth, fruitless and rotten stages, five animal types with idle/walk/eat animations, a Farmer Generator tool, and tool animations. Same license as the rest of the LimeZu line. Paired with Modern User Interface it gives a single-vendor, style-locked farm plus UI plus portrait-generator stack for roughly $8.85, which directly beats the report's own $11 mixed-vendor Route A on cohesion. https://limezu.itch.io/modernfarm

**2. Fonts are absent entirely.** The stack is Phaser plus an HTML/CSS overlay for "all text-heavy UI," so a bundled pixel webfont is a shipping requirement, not a nicety. The report mentions three packs that include .ttf files (Cute Fantasy UI "a .ttf font," Crusenho "Toriko font," Ninja Adventure "2 fonts") and never asks whether the art license covers font redistribution inside a distributed binary. Fonts are separately copyrightable and vendors routinely license them differently. Concrete safe fix: use an SIL Open Font License 1.1 pixel face and self-host it. I verified Silkscreen is OFL 1.1 ("Copyright 2001 The Silkscreen Project Authors," SIL Open Font License Version 1.1) at https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/silkscreen/OFL.txt. Press Start 2P, VT323 and Pixelify Sans sit in the same google/fonts `ofl/` tree. OFL permits bundling in a commercial closed-source app provided you do not sell the font by itself and do not use a Reserved Font Name on a modified copy.

**3. CraftPix prohibits AI/ML use, and the report's "verbatim" prohibited list leaves it out.** The report writes "Prohibited on both tiers, verbatim:" and then lists only the resale clauses. The actual page also prohibits using the assets "for the purposes of training, fine-tuning, developing, testing... any artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML)... systems." todoFarm does not train on the art, so this is almost certainly not a blocker, but a license summary that presents itself as the complete prohibited list and drops the AI clause is unsafe for a product whose defining feature is an LLM NPC. The report flagged Mana Seed's anti-ML clause but did not generalize the pattern, which is now common across pixel-art vendors. https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/

**4. No advice to archive the license text at purchase.** Every itch.io "license" here is free-form description prose that the creator can silently edit or delete at any time. There is no versioned EULA, no license file in most downloads, and the itch.io receipt does not capture the terms. For an app the user intends to distribute commercially, the report should have said: screenshot or PDF the license block on the day you buy, store it with the receipt, and re-check before each release. This is the single cheapest risk mitigation available and it is missing.

**5. Tauri packaging versus the "no redistribution" clauses is never connected.** A Tauri .app bundles assets as ordinary files under `Contents/Resources`, extractable by anyone who right-clicks Show Package Contents. The report notices this issue only for CraftPix ("the only marketplace license here that explicitly contemplates embedding assets such that end users cannot extract them") and for Kenmi via the comment thread, then drops it. It applies equally to Sprout Lands, Cozy Farm, Serial, Mana Seed and every LimeZu pack, all of which forbid redistribution without defining it. The right move is to pack sprites into a texture atlas rather than ship named source PNGs, and the report should have said so given it knows the stack.

**6. Two packs are cited as verified but never analyzed.** Tiny Swords (Pixel Frog) and Kenney Tiny Dungeon both appear in the "Verified by fetching the primary page" list and in Sources, but neither appears anywhere in the body. Tiny Swords is materially relevant: I confirmed its license reads "Feel free to use this asset pack in both personal and commercial projects, modifying the assets as needed. Crediting is not required... You may not redistribute, resell, or repackage the assets, even if the files are modified." Commercial-safe, credit-free, and dropped without explanation. https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords

**7. The 16x16 farm-pack field is under-surveyed.** One itch.io tag page I fetched surfaces several the report never names: RareOnion "2D Life Farm Simulator Asset-pack (16x16)" at $19.99, Anokolisa "World Building" at 32x32 for $22.50, WinLu Farm Tilesheet at $7 (30 percent off), teaceratops "GameBoy Style Farm and Town Tileset" at $5. The report's own caveat that its search was incomplete is honest, but it presents a two-route decision as if the field had been swept. https://itch.io/game-assets/tag-farm/tag-pixel-art

**8. Nothing is evaluated for Phaser specifically.** No pack is checked for Tiled-compatible .tmx/.tsx output, for tile margin and spacing, or for edge extrusion to prevent atlas bleeding at fractional camera zoom, which is the most common visual bug in a Phaser tilemap. LimeZu explicitly advertises autotiles for Godot and GameMaker, not Phaser or Tiled, and the report repeats that detail without drawing the conclusion.

**9. The emergent-chest mechanic is under-served by the recommendation.** Chest categories are invented at runtime by the NPC and are open-ended, so the app needs many visually distinct chests, or one chest with a programmatic recolor scheme, or a chest plus an overlaid category icon. Route A's answer is Serial's single 4-frame chest, which cannot distinguish N categories. karsiori's 9 distinct chests are dismissed for being oversized when they are arguably the better structural fit, and the report never raises palette-swapping as the obvious solution. Related and unflagged: Serial's chest is 18x16, so it overhangs a 16x16 tile grid by 2px, which matters for tilemap alignment in Phaser.

**10. No license is checked for a "games only" scope.** todoFarm is a productivity app, not a game. Ninja Adventure's grant reads "in your own games." Because that pack is CC0 the wording is inert, but the report never asks the question, and it would matter for a non-CC0 pack worded the same way.

## Net assessment

**Trustworthy on the thing that matters most, sloppy at the edges, and materially incomplete.**

Every license quote I spot-checked against the live page came back accurate, and I checked twenty of them. The core legal conclusions are sound: pay for the premium tiers, the free tiers of Sprout Lands and Cozy Farm genuinely forbid commercial use, Kenney and Ninja Adventure and karsiori are genuinely CC0, Crusenho is genuinely CC BY 4.0 with a real ongoing attribution obligation, Mana Seed is genuinely per-product, LPC is genuinely copyleft. I found **no case where a pack claimed commercial-safe is actually non-commercial**, which was the highest-stakes failure mode. Call the license research roughly 90 percent reliable.

The errors that exist are concentrated in three places. One real price error (Modern Exteriors, inverted). One headline flag that misdescribes a license and contradicts the report's own quote three sections later (Cute Fantasy RPG free tier). And two instances of citing weak or wrong-scope evidence as if it were authoritative (a creator's itch.io comment presented as page text, a paid bundle page presented as a general storefront license).

The bigger problem is not error but incompleteness, and the report partly knows it. The missing LimeZu Modern Farm invalidates the "Route A versus Route B" framing, because a cheaper and more cohesive third option existed at the same vendor the report was already recommending. The total absence of font licensing is a hole in a stack the report was told is HTML/CSS-overlay based. And nothing in the report is evaluated against Phaser or against Tauri packaging, despite both being fixed project constraints.

Actionable verdict: use the report's license findings, fix the four corrections above, replace the Route A tileset with LimeZu Modern Farm or at minimum price it against Kenmi, add an OFL pixel font, and re-do the chest selection with the emergent-category requirement actually in scope.
