THE RULES YOU SET

  • 9 chests, hard cap. At 9 the classifier stops creating and starts fitting. It may rename or merge its own chests to make room.
  • Your chests are locked. A chest you made by hand can never be renamed, merged, or deleted by the AI. It can only file into it.
  • The AI never speaks. No prompts, no approvals, no clarifying questions. It files and it tags. You correct it by dragging.
  • One timer, ever. The furnace is the only place work happens.

THE ECONOMY

  • 12 coins a tomato, plus a 50% bonus at 3 tomatoes or more. Long grinds pay better than scattered ones.
  • Finishing a work item also forges a decoration tied to that task. Your farm is a record of what you actually did.
  • Leisure costs 1 coin a minute. 30 minutes of Minecraft is 30 coins, about two and a half tomatoes of real work.
  • Coins are only earned in the furnace. Ticking a box without burning time pays nothing.

STILL FAKED

  • Every sprite is drawn procedurally so nothing is licensed yet. Ships on a real pack.
  • Classification is scripted. Real one is a small flash-class model, swappable, terse JSON out.
  • Nothing saves. Real app is SQLite on your Mac.
  • Walking, collision, chest proximity and focus handoff are real. Arrow keys or WASD.
