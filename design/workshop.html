TODOFARM ART STANDARD v0.1

One base unit: the 16×16 tile. Every canvas is a whole multiple of it, which is what makes atlas packing, collision, and camera zoom stay integer. Copy Minecraft's discipline here: the constraint is what lets a hundred artists produce art that looks like one game.

HARD RULES, ENFORCED BY THE VALIDATOR

  • PNG-32 only. Indexed PNG and JPG are rejected on sight.
  • Binary alpha. Every pixel is either fully opaque or fully transparent. No soft edges, ever. Semi-transparent pixels break atlas bleeding and read as blur at 3× zoom.
  • Exact canvas. Not "about 16 wide". Exactly the size the slot names.
  • Colour cap per class. Counted as distinct RGB among fully opaque pixels only. A transparent pixel is not a colour, and the same RGB at two alpha values is not two colours. This rule is written down because the identical file passes or fails depending on it.
  • No baked shadow. The engine draws contact shadows so lighting stays consistent. A shadow you paint in cannot be turned off.
  • Frames are a vertical strip. Frame 1 on top. Height must divide exactly by the frame count, and no frame may be blank.
  • Export straight from Aseprite is fine. Validation runs in your browser on decoded RGBA, so an indexed PNG carrying transparency in a tRNS chunk passes. Naive server-side checks reject those exports; this one does not.

SOFT RULES, REVIEWED BY A HUMAN

  • 1px outlines maximum, and darker than the fill rather than pure black.
  • Light comes from the upper left. Every prop, no exceptions.
  • No dithering below 2×2 blocks. It shimmers when the camera moves.
  • Read the silhouette first. If it is unrecognisable as a solid black shape at 16×16, it will not read on the farm either.
  • Stay in the game palette where you can. It is a guide, not a jail, and the validator only warns.
